Marina Trajkovska

New York · AI-Native Technical Operator · Applied AI @ Vellum

Hi, I'm Marina! Foodie, dog mom, new to New York, Knicks fan, and an AI-native technical operator who loves helping every function of a business actually use AI day to day.

I started out in QA, then ran my own AI studio building LLM workflows and sales systems for founders. Along the way, I built a career agent and founder-led GTM engine end to end as founding engineer at Odyseek and helped clients figure out where AI actually belongs as a solutions architect at Bubble.

These days I'm at Vellum, where I partner with customers as a Solutions Architect (Applied AI) and embed across Finance, Customer Success, Content, GTM, and Engineering to ship production agents, automate our release pipeline, and enable non-technical teammates to author their own skills.
