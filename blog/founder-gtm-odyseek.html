LLM sentiment and Fathom GTM engine (+30% signups)

Case study · Founder-Led GTM

GTM Enablement AI

How building a Monday-morning content workflow with Odyseek's CEO helped her go from posting anxiety to publishing 3x+ a week on LinkedIn, lifting signups by ~30%.

The problem

At Odyseek, founder-led posts on LinkedIn were one of our best channels for bringing in both individual users and mid-market companies. Our CEO knew the HR and career space inside out and shared great ideas every day on internal calls and client meetings.

Still, staring at a blank LinkedIn page gave her real posting anxiety. Between running the company and taking consultative sales calls, she didn't have time to track what people were talking about across HR social media or turn her spoken thoughts into posts every week.

What we built

Working closely with her, I built a workflow that listened to her real conversations during the week and combined them with live HR market sentiment every Monday morning:

  1. Pulling meeting notes from Fathom: Instead of asking the CEO to brainstorm from scratch, the tool pulled her meeting notes and transcripts from Fathom across internal meetings and customer calls, picking up the exact phrases and stories she used naturally.
  2. Saving bookmarks all week: Whenever she read an interesting post or article during the week, she saved the bookmark to the tool so it fed right into her weekly queue.
  3. Checking HR sentiment with GPT-5: Using GPT-5, the tool looked at what people in the HR and recruiting world were debating online and matched those topics with what she was already talking about in meetings.
  4. Monday-morning topics and LinkedIn drafts: Every Monday morning, the tool gave her a short list of topics to talk about along with ready-to-edit LinkedIn post drafts written in her own voice.

The result

  • Posting 3x+ a week on LinkedIn: Removed blank-page anxiety completely and helped our CEO post at least three times every week.
  • ~30% more top-of-funnel signups: Her steady posting cadence drove roughly 30% more organic signups for our platform.
  • More mid-market sales calls: Her LinkedIn posts also brought in more consultative calls, which was her main way of closing mid-market companies.
