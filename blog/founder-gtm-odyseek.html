Turning Fathom meeting notes and HR sentiment into a founder-led LinkedIn engine (+30% signups)

Case study · Founder-Led GTM

GTM Enablement AI

How co-building an AI content workflow with Odyseek's CEO took her from social-posting anxiety to publishing 3x+ a week on LinkedIn, lifting top-of-funnel signups by ~30%.

The Challenge

At Odyseek, founder-led marketing on LinkedIn was one of our highest-leverage channels for bringing both individual users and mid-market companies into the funnel. Our CEO had deep domain expertise in the HR and career ecosystem, and she shared sharp insights constantly on internal calls and client meetings.

However, staring at a blank LinkedIn composer created real posting anxiety. Between running the company and taking consultative sales calls, she didn't have the bandwidth to track what was trending across HR Twitter/LinkedIn or turn her spoken thoughts into polished posts every week.

What We Built: The Monday-Morning Founder Content Engine

Working closely with her, I built an automated workflow that captured her real voice during the week and paired it with live market sentiment every Monday morning:

  1. Listening to Real Conversations via Fathom: Instead of asking the CEO to sit down and brainstorm from scratch, the pipeline ingested meeting notes and transcripts from Fathom across our internal product syncs and customer conversations, extracting the exact phrases, stories, and convictions she used naturally.
  2. Capturing Weekly Bookmarks: Whenever she came across an interesting article or post during the week, she forwarded the bookmark to the tool so her current reading fed directly into her content queue.
  3. GPT-5 Sentiment Analysis Across the HR Ecosystem: Using GPT-5, the workflow analyzed current sentiment and debates happening across the HR and recruiting space online, identifying where her perspective offered a sharp, contrarian, or practical take.
  4. Monday Morning Topic Briefs & LinkedIn Drafts: Every Monday morning, the system surfaced a curated set of high-signal topics along with ready-to-edit LinkedIn post drafts written in her own voice.

Results & Impact

  • Consistent 3x+ weekly publishing cadence: Eliminated blank-page anxiety completely, taking the CEO from sporadic posting to publishing at least three high-conviction LinkedIn posts every week.
  • ~30% lift in top-of-funnel signups: The steady cadence of founder-led content drove an estimated 30% increase in organic signups for our HR and career platform.
  • More mid-market consultative sales calls: Beyond self-serve signups, her LinkedIn presence directly increased inbound consultative calls, which served as her primary sales motion for bringing larger mid-market companies into the pipeline.
