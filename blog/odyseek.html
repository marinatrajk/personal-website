Rebuilding Odyseek into a full-stack career agent, voice coach, and soft-skill AI ATS

Case study · Founding Engineer @ Odyseek

Agents AI Product

How I took Odyseek from broken OCR resume imports on no-code to 100% parsing accuracy, rebuilt the platform in TypeScript and React as a chat and voice career agent, and built an AI ATS that screens 500 to 1,000 resumes for soft skills like grit.

Fixing broken resume imports (OCR + LLMs for 100% accuracy)

When I joined Odyseek as founding engineer, the platform was struggling with its very first step: reading a user's resume. It relied on outdated OCR techniques that scrambled sections together, and the app had a maze of slow, fragile workflows just to sort a candidate's job history by date.

My first optimization was pairing OCR with LLMs to read the raw text and write clean, structured data on import. Combining OCR extraction with an LLM structuring pass brought our resume import accuracy to 100% and eliminated the slow date-sorting workarounds overnight.

Auditing every part of a resume with AI

Once we could import resumes cleanly, I built targeted AI checks to review the actual quality of a resume line by line:

  • Checking pronoun usage and active voice across bullets.
  • Evaluating whether the wording and impact statements were clear.
  • Counting the number of bullet points per role so sections stayed balanced.
  • Checking layout formatting, summary length, and the right number of skills.

Rebuilding from Bubble into a TypeScript & React career agent

Originally, Odyseek was built on Bubble (no-code) and stitched together with several external tools, including Vellum for running our AI workflows. As the product grew, I decided to rebuild the entire platform from the ground up in TypeScript and React and bring all of our AI workflows in-house in one codebase.

Instead of filling out forms, users interacted with a simple, smart chatbot:

  • Drop in a resume, get a full breakdown: You upload your resume in chat and the agent automatically runs all formatting, wording, and bullet-count checks.
  • Role suggestions & tailored rewrites: The agent suggests roles that fit your background and rewrites your resume for the exact role you want to target.
  • Voice interview & value-story coach: I also built a voice agent where candidates could practice live mock interviews and rehearse how they tell their personal value story out loud.

Building an AI ATS that scores soft skills across 500 to 1,000 resumes

Alongside the candidate app, I built an AI-powered Applicant Tracking System (ATS) that helped companies like Mike's Hot Honey hire much faster by analyzing batches of 500 to 1,000 resumes at once.

Most ATS tools only look for exact keyword matches. We designed ours to read between the lines and score soft skills, sentiment, and custom hiring criteria:

  • Detecting grit from career trajectory: In one batch, a candidate had worked at Yahoo for over 20 years. A traditional filter might write that off as staying in one place too long, but our agent flagged him as high-grit because it traced how he steadily advanced through different roles and took on bigger responsibilities across those 20+ years.
  • Custom trait scoring: Hiring teams could type in specific traits and criteria they cared about for a role, and the ATS scored every resume on how many of those traits showed up in the candidate's actual history.
