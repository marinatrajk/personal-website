From AI-anxious to 1 GEO article a day: enabling our Head of Content to author production skills

Case study · Content & GEO Enablement

Agents Enablement GTM

How hands-on AI literacy workshops and a meta-skill for structuring SKILL.md turned a non-technical content leader into an autonomous builder shipping one GEO article per day end-to-end.

Context: AI Literacy Beyond Building a Tool

When our Head of Content wanted to scale our GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) process, he hit the wall every non-technical builder hits. Agent skills look simple from the outside, just markdown prompts and files, but making an agent execute a multi-step editorial methodology reliably means knowing where models actually break. Instructions drift. Context gets ignored. Steps fail silently.

True enablement isn't building a black-box tool for someone and walking away; it's teaching them how to structure their own domain expertise so their agent can do a great job.

Hands-On Workshops & Designing the Meta-Skill

I ran hands-on workshops with him to break down his editorial and GEO methodology, map out the hierarchy and outline of his skills, and pair that training with a meta-skill I built that distills the guardrails experienced builders apply by hand into five rules:

  1. Triggers written in the user’s words. If a skill describes itself in abstract terms instead of the exact way the operator asks for a GEO audit or draft, the model never invokes it reliably.
  2. Warnings at the point of action. Gotchas and editorial guardrails live inline at the step they apply to, not buried in a footnote at the end. By line 240, the model has forgotten line 10.
  3. Checkpoints between steps. Multi-step GEO research and drafting skills pause and verify state between outline, citation check, and final draft before continuing.
  4. Branching where the decision happens. If/then logic sits inside the step it belongs to, not in a centralized error-handling section the model won’t scan ahead to read.
  5. A definition of done. Every skill ends with a clear completion signal so the workflow stops cleanly when the article is ready to publish.

Together, we translated his manual GEO playbook into a clean hierarchy of skills, including the public GEO Writing skill on Vellum, that his agent could run with end-to-end.

Results & Impact

  • From AI-anxious to 1 GEO article per day: After our hands-on sessions and skill-structuring workshops, he went from feeling anxious about relying on AI to publishing one high-quality GEO article per day autonomously using his GEO writing skill and end-to-end workflow.
  • Reusable meta-skill across the company: The same meta-skill guardrails now allow anyone on the team (from Content to Finance) to turn their domain knowledge into production-ready agent skills without waiting on engineering.
