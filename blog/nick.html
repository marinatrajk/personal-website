Helping our Head of Content ship 1 GEO article a day with a meta-skill

Case study · Content & GEO Enablement

Agents Enablement GTM

How hands-on workshops and a simple meta-skill helped a non-technical teammate go from feeling anxious about AI to publishing one GEO article a day on his own.

The problem

Our Head of Content wanted to use AI to scale our GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) articles, but he felt anxious about how to actually make it work.

Agent skills look easy from the outside because they are just markdown files. In reality, getting an AI agent to follow a multi-step writing process without losing track is tricky. If the instructions aren't structured well, the model skips steps, forgets earlier rules, or writes generic content.

I didn't want to just build a black-box tool for him and walk away. I wanted to teach him how to turn his own writing playbook into skills that his agent could run reliably.

What we did

We sat down for hands-on workshops where we broke down how he researches, outlines, and edits an article. Then we mapped out a clear outline and hierarchy for his skills.

To make sure he could keep building new skills on his own, I also built a meta-skill (a skill that helps you write good skills) based on five simple rules:

  1. Use the words you actually type. Write the skill trigger using the exact phrasing you use in chat when asking for a GEO audit or draft.
  2. Put warnings right where they happen. Don't bury important rules at the bottom of the file. Put them right inside the step where the agent needs to follow them.
  3. Check the work between steps. Have the agent pause and verify its work between the outline, the source check, and the final draft before moving on.
  4. Keep "if/then" rules inside the step. Tell the agent how to handle edge cases right where the decision happens, instead of in a separate section at the end.
  5. Be clear about what "done" means. End every skill with a clear finish line so the agent knows when the article is ready to publish.

Using this setup, we turned his manual GEO process into a set of skills his agent runs end-to-end, including the public GEO Writing skill on Vellum.

The result

  • 1 GEO article a day: He went from feeling anxious about AI to publishing one high-quality GEO article every day on his own using his GEO writing skill.
  • A meta-skill the whole team uses: Anyone on the team, from Content to Finance, can now use the same meta-skill to turn their own daily work into agent skills without waiting on engineering.
