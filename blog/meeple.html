Enabling sales reps to follow up with custom pages for their leads

Case study · Meeple

GTM Enablement Agents

How we turned messy post-call emails and static PDFs into tailored, interactive client microportals generated in 30 seconds from a Zoom call.

The problem

After every discovery or demo call, sales reps were spending 30 to 45 minutes writing long recap emails and attaching four or five separate PDFs, slide decks, and links. Every rep had their own patchwork system: some sent Notion pages, some sent Loom links, and others sent a three-sentence email with a generic deck.

Worse, B2B deals rarely close with just the person on the first call. When an internal champion tried to forward a sprawling email thread to their VP, CFO, or security team for budget sign-off, half the context got lost. New stakeholders joined late in the cycle asking the same questions, deals stalled in mid-funnel, and the sales team had zero visibility into whether anyone on the buyer’s side was actually reading their proposals.

What we built

We built Meeple to turn a Zoom call into a personalized, interactive buyer microportal in under 30 seconds, giving reps a single link tailored to that specific lead and giving champions a hub they can share internally with decision-makers.

Step How it works
Call ingestion & extraction Reps upload a Zoom recording (audio/video or transcript) right after a call. The pipeline transcribes the conversation and extracts the prospect’s role, whether they’re the sole decision-maker, their core pain points, the relevant value proposition, and agreed-upon next steps.
Live account research Alongside the transcript, an AI research workflow scrapes the lead’s company website and runs grounded web search to pull company overview, recent initiatives, and role-specific priorities, combining what was said on the call with real account intelligence.
Tailored microportal generation Using the extracted deal context and research brief, the system generates a branded page structured around the buyer: a personalized intro, key challenges in the prospect’s own words, a tailored “Why Us” business case, an executive champion note for internal forwarding, and an interactive Mutual Action Plan with assignees and due dates.
Modular saved sections Reps can attach pre-approved collateral blocks in one click (such as SOC 2 security packets, pricing tiers, ROI case studies, 2-minute product tours, or 14-day onboarding timelines) and fine-tune any block inline before publishing.
Buyer intent & pipeline tracking Every microportal tracks views and clicks across stakeholders and syncs directly with a deal pipeline board (Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Won), so reps can see when a portal is shared internally and which sections decision-makers care about most.

Impact & takeaways

  • 45 minutes down to 30 seconds: Post-call follow-up went from a tedious manual chore that reps procrastinated on to an instant review-and-send workflow right after hanging up.
  • Arming the internal champion: Expecting a prospect to pitch your product from memory using a generic slide deck is where deals go to die. Giving them a page written in their company’s own language, complete with a champion summary for their VP or CFO, made internal selling effortless.
  • AI synthesis + modular guardrails beat blank-canvas generation: Combining AI-generated deal narratives (from the call transcript and live account research) with pre-approved Saved Sections (pricing, security, case studies) gave reps speed and personalization without ever going off-brand.
