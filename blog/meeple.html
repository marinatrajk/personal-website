Building an LLM harness for custom buyer follow-up pages (+5% close rate)

Case study · Sales Enablement (Meeple)

GTM Enablement Agents

How I built a custom data-scraping, prioritization, and frontend follow-up harness at my AI studio (before modern agent skills existed) to help our CEO and sales team move more deals from discovery into evaluation.

The Problem

At my AI studio, our CEO and sales team needed a sharper way to follow up after discovery calls: capturing the exact buyer words used on the call, understanding what the prospect's company was actually doing, and tailoring our sales motion to show how our services fit their specific workflows.

After every discovery or demo call, reps were spending 30 to 45 minutes writing long recap emails and attaching static PDFs or decks. Worse, when an internal champion forwarded a sprawling email thread to their executive team, half the context got lost, and deals stalled between discovery and evaluation.

Engineering the Harness (Early AI, Pre-Skills Era)

Because we built Meeple during the early wave of AI before modern agent skills or MCP frameworks existed, I had to engineer the entire orchestration harness from scratch: scraping account data, prioritizing buyer signals, wrapping the LLM chatbot logic, and mapping the structured output directly into a custom frontend design.

Layer How It Works
Call ingestion & buyer language extraction Ingests the discovery call recording or transcript to identify the buyer's exact words, their role, whether they are the sole decision-maker, their core pain points, and agreed-upon next steps.
Account scraping & signal prioritization Scrapes a wide set of company data and web signals, then filters and prioritizes what the prospect's business is doing right now so the sales team can customize the pitch around their actual roadmap.
Custom LLM harness & frontend mapping Runs the prioritized account intelligence and call transcript through a custom-built prompt harness that maps structured outputs directly to interactive frontend components: a personalized intro, challenges in the buyer's own words, a tailored business case, and a Mutual Action Plan.
Modular collateral & pipeline tracking Lets reps attach pre-approved case studies, pricing tiers, and onboarding timelines in one click, while tracking stakeholder views as deals progress from Discovery to Evaluation.

Results & Takeaways

  • Closed 5% more deals: By customizing our follow-up motion around the buyer's exact words and prioritized company context, we moved significantly more prospects from discovery into active evaluation and increased deal close rates by 5%.
  • 45 minutes down to 30 seconds: Post-call follow-up went from a manual research and copywriting chore to an instant review-and-send microportal right after hanging up.
  • Building the harness before the frameworks existed: Writing our own data-scraping, prioritization, and UI-mapping harness early on laid the foundation for how I now architect modern agent skills and GTM workflows.
